Research Plan
=============

The project research plan is centered around a few key goals:

- Evaluate CfC and NCP backbones on Split-MNIST and Split-CIFAR-10
- Fix the Tennessee Eastman Process benchmark and validate temporal learning
- Run wiring and dynamics ablation studies for H1 and H2
- Generate publication-ready tables and figures from WandB results
- Produce a final paper draft with honest empirical claims

Available commands:

.. code-block:: bash

   make run-tep
   make run-ablations
   make analyze
   make paper

The full plan is also captured in ``RESEARCH_PLAN.md``.
